## AE-01 — What does soon mean?

**Pack:** University & Everyday Life  
**Skill:** Clarifying expectations and capacity  
**Skill pair:** AE-PAIR-01  
**Broad rationale:** R2, R3

**Situation and your constraints**

You are working on a group presentation. Your slides can be finished by 6 p.m., but not earlier. You do not yet know when the group needs them.

**Your goal:** Establish a clear deadline and communicate what you can realistically provide.

**Opening message:**

> Maya: “Can you finish your slides soon?”

### Decision 1

**AE-01-D1-C3 — +0 energy**

- **You say:** Sure, I will have everything ready whenever you need it.
- **Reply:** Maya: “Okay, I will expect the slides at four.”
- **Why:** That promises unspecified availability despite your known time constraint.
- **Next:** AE-01-R1

**AE-01-D1-C1 — +1 energy**

- **You say:** What time do you need them? I can finish by six.
- **Reply:** Maya: “There are two stages to what I need.”
- **Why:** You ask for a deadline and state a feasible completion time.
- **Next:** AE-01-REVEAL

**AE-01-D1-C2 — +1 energy**

- **You say:** I can finish by six. Does that work for combining the presentation?
- **Reply:** Maya: “I need something earlier for the layout.”
- **Why:** You check whether your available time meets the practical need.
- **Next:** AE-01-REVEAL

**Guided repair AE-01-R1 — unscored**

Only offered after the zero-credit choice; the player may pass instead.

- **You say:** I should clarify: I can finish by six. Do you need something earlier?
- **Reply:** Maya: “Let me explain the two deadlines.”
- **Next:** AE-01-REVEAL

**New information before decision two**

Maya explains: “I need a rough version by four to combine everyone’s work. Final edits can wait until six.” You can send an outline by four, but not a full rough version.

### Decision 2

**AE-01-D2-C1 — +1 energy**

- **You say:** I can send an outline by four and finished slides by six. Would that work?
- **Reply:** Maya: “Yes, the outline is enough to start the layout.”
- **Why:** You propose a feasible alternative and check whether it meets the need.
- **Next:** AE-01-END

**AE-01-D2-C2 — +1 energy**

- **You say:** I cannot provide a full draft by four. Could you start the layout from my outline?
- **Reply:** Maya: “Yes, send the outline and I will start.”
- **Why:** You state the limit and negotiate how to divide the work.
- **Next:** AE-01-END

**AE-01-D2-C3 — +0 energy**

- **You say:** I will send the full draft by four.
- **Reply:** Maya: “Great, I will plan around that.”
- **Why:** The commitment conflicts with your actual ability to provide a full draft.
- **Next:** AE-01-R2

**Guided repair AE-01-R2 — unscored**

Only offered after the zero-credit choice; the player may pass instead.

- **You say:** I need to revise that: an outline by four, finished slides by six. Does that work?
- **Reply:** Maya: “Yes, I can work from the outline.”
- **Next:** AE-01-END

### Closing feedback

The useful result is a shared, feasible plan. A positive reaction from someone else does not make an unrealistic promise feasible.

**Reusable phrase:** I can provide ___ by ___. Would that meet what you need?

**Round energy:** 1 completion + first-decision credit + second-decision credit. Repairs add 0; Pass gives 1 total.

- **Next:** AE-02-R2

**AE-02-D2-C1 — +1 energy**

- **You say:** I can arrange the chairs. Can you handle the papers and clear the table?
- **Reply:** Nadia: “Yes, I will do the table.”
- **Why:** You propose a division that uses each person’s available information.
- **Next:** AE-02-END

**Guided repair AE-02-R2 — unscored**

Only offered after the zero-credit choice; the player may pass instead.

- **You say:** I need you to identify the papers to keep. I will start with the chairs.
- **Reply:** Nadia: “I will sort the papers now.”
- **Next:** AE-02-END

### Closing feedback

A household request can hide both a deadline and a decision only another person can make. Clarifying those allows a practical division of work.

**Reusable phrase:** Which part should I handle, and what do I need to know first?

**Round energy:** 1 completion + first-decision credit + second-decision credit. Repairs add 0; Pass gives 1 total.

**Guided repair AE-03-R2 — unscored**

Only offered after the zero-credit choice; the player may pass instead.

- **Reply:** Sam: “Agreed.”
- **Next:** AE-03-END

### Closing feedback

**Reusable phrase:** That works for me if ___. What should we do if it changes?

**Round energy:** 1 completion + first-decision credit + second-decision credit. Repairs add 0; Pass gives 1 total.
